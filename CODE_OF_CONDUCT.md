# Fajrix community code of conduct

Fajrix welcomes constructive participation from people with different backgrounds, experience and perspectives. These standards apply to participation in FajrixUK repositories that adopt this policy, including issues, pull requests, discussions and related community communications. They also apply when someone represents Fajrix in an official community capacity.

## Participation standards

Treat people with respect. Discuss ideas and evidence, explain disagreements clearly, and give others a fair opportunity to respond. Acknowledge corrections and recognise the work of others. Keep contributions relevant and accessible to the people taking part.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, personal attacks and deliberate intimidation are unacceptable. This includes targeting people because of characteristics such as disability, age, race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, sex, gender identity or sexual orientation. Publishing private information without permission, impersonation, spam and retaliation against someone who raises a concern are also unacceptable.

Good-faith criticism, differing interpretations and requests for evidence are welcome. Disagreement alone is not misconduct.

## Reporting a concern

Contact [info@fajrix.com](mailto:info@fajrix.com) with the subject **GitHub conduct concern**. Include the relevant repository, links or dates, a concise account of what happened and any immediate concerns. Share only the personal information needed to assess the report. Avoid posting sensitive allegations in public issues.

Reports are considered by Fajrix maintainers. Information is shared only as needed to assess and address the concern, or where disclosure is required by law. If a report concerns a maintainer, identify that conflict so it can be considered in how the report is handled.

## Moderation and review

Maintainers assess the conduct, context, impact and any previous incidents. Responses may include clarification, a private warning, removal of inappropriate content, temporary participation restrictions or a permanent ban. Serious threats or abuse may require immediate action. Responses should be proportionate and reasons explained where appropriate without exposing private information.

A participant may request reconsideration through the same email address, explaining the decision being challenged and any relevant additional information. Maintainers will consider that information and communicate the outcome where appropriate.
