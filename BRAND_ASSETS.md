## UK trade mark status

**Fajrix® is a registered trade mark in the United Kingdom.** Fajrix is the brand of Fajrix Limited, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 15001279).

The registration statement concerns the Fajrix trade mark in the UK. It does not claim registration in other jurisdictions or imply that each logo, monogram, design element or variation has a separate registration.

## Assets and intended use

The Fajrix name, FJ monogram, wordmark, logos and banners identify the Fajrix business. Artwork in the [assets directory](assets/) is published for use in Fajrix's own GitHub profile and other authorised materials.

Public access to a file, including GitHub's ability to display or fork a repository, is **not** a general licence to reuse, adapt, distribute or commercially exploit Fajrix brand artwork. See the [repository rights and reuse notice](LICENSE.md) for existing permissions and legal rights.

## Permission requests

For uses requiring permission, contact [info@fajrix.com](mailto:info@fajrix.com) with the relevant asset, intended use, placement, audience, territory and proposed alterations. Permissions for one purpose do not imply permissions for other purposes.

When use is authorised, follow the specific terms supplied. Preserve the mark's proportions, legibility and approved presentation, and do not imply that an independent project, product or publication is operated by, affiliated with, approved by or endorsed by Fajrix without permission.

## Accurate references

Factual references to Fajrix and uses permitted under applicable law, GitHub's terms or separate permissions remain subject to those existing rights. The registration symbol **®** may be used to identify the registered Fajrix mark where appropriate; it should not be used to suggest an unregistered mark or unrelated mark has been registered.
