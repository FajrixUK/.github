# Support and contact

For a correction or proposal relating to a public repository, use that repository's issue forms and contribution guidance. Include the affected page or file and enough context to understand the question.

For business enquiries, collaboration or a conversation that should remain private, contact [info@fajrix.com](mailto:info@fajrix.com). Please identify the relevant repository or publication. Product information is available at [fajrix.com](https://fajrix.com).

Security concerns follow the [private security reporting process](https://github.com/FajrixUK/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md). Conduct concerns follow the [code of conduct](https://github.com/FajrixUK/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

Public issues are visible to others. Do not include personal financial information, customer records, credentials or confidential business material. Public repository discussions do not provide account-specific support or a guaranteed response time.
